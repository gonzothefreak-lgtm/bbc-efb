# Aircraft profile drawing sources

These are exact source side views from Boeing’s airport-planning manuals. The app uses cropped excerpts. Blue drawings were recoloured for the EFB contrast and isolated from black annotations where possible. Monochrome drawings retain relevant source linework and may include dimension labels. The model and ICAO designator are separate. A blank drawing means an exact view is unavailable.

[Boeing Airport Planning Manuals](https://www.boeing.com/commercial/airports/plan-manuals) · [ICAO Doc 8643](https://www.icao.int/operational-safety/doc-8643-aircraft-type-designators/search)

The owner reported Boeing’s permission for this use. The source drawings remain credited to Boeing.
