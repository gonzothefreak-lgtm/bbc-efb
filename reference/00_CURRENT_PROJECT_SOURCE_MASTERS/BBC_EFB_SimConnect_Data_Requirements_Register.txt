SCOPE AND DESIGN CONSTRAINTS (FROM AGREED SIMCONNECT DESIGN REVIEW)
- MSFS 2020 is the initial target; assess MSFS 2024 compatibility separately.
- Initial integration is local; retain future network connection capability.
- Read-only simulator telemetry by default. Do not implement aircraft control commands. Any possible vPilot-related exception requires separate investigation and explicit approval.
- Request only data used by active consumers; share subscriptions across consumers and use appropriate update rates.
- Background consumers (for example flight logging or streaming overlays) may continue while the EFB window is minimised.
- Support simulator absence, disconnects, reconnects, pauses, stale/unavailable data and clean shutdown.
- Prefer standard SimConnect data where available; aircraft-specific plugins are optional and require validation.
- Log diagnostic information locally with appropriate retention and optional debug mode.

CRITICAL MEANINGS AND RESEARCH RISKS
- PLANE TOUCHDOWN NORMAL VELOCITY: last touchdown velocity along ground normal, reportedly ft/s; |ft/s| × 60 may yield an absolute ft/min magnitude, but the actual sign, freshness, event association, bounce handling and compatibility need live validation. Do not assign an old touchdown reading to a new landing, treat VS as impact velocity or advertise touchdown G without evidence.
- TITLE/ATC TYPE/ATC MODEL/ATC ID and ATC FLIGHT NUMBER do not guarantee a canonical aircraft type, registration or VATSIM callsign; allow pilot correction. Aircraft-livery strings must not change aircraft-type grouping.
- Latitude/longitude and ground state are observations, not authoritative origin/destination ICAO or completed flight phases. Airport matching needs an approved reference/manual confirmation and ambiguity handling.
- Parking-brake released / engine running / moving / parking spot are separate signals, not automatically OFF BLOCK or ON BLOCK.
- ZULU TIME, ABSOLUTE TIME, SIMULATION TIME, SIMULATION RATE, application UTC receipt time and monotonic host time answer different questions. Rules for pauses, Active Pause, sim acceleration and credited flight time remain UNAPPROVED.
- SimConnect payload stations do NOT categorise actual crew, PAX, baggage and freight or justify arbitrary person-weight assumptions. Pilot-entered or trusted import data may be necessary; incomplete totals should never appear as precise actuals.
- No high-rate always-on flight-track recorder, touchdown sampling frequency, arbitrary aircraft plugin transport or raw telemetry retention duration is authorised.

CORE-TO-RECORDER QUALITY / SUBSCRIPTION INVESTIGATION (NOT SELECTED)
- Proposed value metadata: canonical name/type/unit, original source/provider, simulator UTC/context plus host receipt/monotonic time, data/session timestamp, availability/freshness (LIVE, STALE, UNAVAILABLE under field-specific tolerance), and gap/change/capability flags. Keep observed, derived, edited and imported provenance separate.
- Candidate subscription behaviour: identity on load/change; movement and event context at a justifiable low overhead cadence; event-correlation or short landing-window burst only if proven needed; engine/parking/weight lower priority and on-demand; keep essential Recorder data supplied when minimised but reduce visual-only updates.
- Proposed Core recovery: report stale/unavailable observations, gaps, aircraft/session changes, pause and adapter capability/failure; no silent interpolation of missing flight facts. Recorder controls persisted log state and recovery.
- Specific rates, callback/dispatch strategy, freshness thresholds, exact contract and queue/back-pressure mechanisms remain open.
